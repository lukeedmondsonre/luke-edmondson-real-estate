How Much Income Do You Need to Buy a House in Chico, CA?

Interest rates and affordability are the first questions most Chico buyers ask. Here is what a typical Chico purchase costs per month, and the income that goes with it.

A buyer needs roughly $91,000 in annual income to afford a typical Chico home right now, according to Redfin data for January 2026, while the median household income in Chico is about $74,000. That gap is the real starting point for anyone shopping here, and it is why I like to talk about affordability before we ever look at a listing.

How Much Income Do You Need to Buy a House in Chico?

Plan on roughly $91,000 a year for a typical Chico home. Redfin's January 2026 figures show that income level would put about 36.9 percent of a median earner's pay toward housing, and only about 21.3 percent of Chico listings were affordable to a household earning the local median.

Two things are worth knowing. First, the Census Bureau reports a lower median household income for Chico, $67,929 in its 2019 to 2023 estimates, so different sources land in different places. Second, your own number depends on your debts, down payment, credit, and loan type. A lender looks at your monthly debts compared with your gross income, and the limit varies by loan program. The only way to know your number is a conversation with a lender, and we can sit down and line that up before you start touring homes.

Why Chico Looks Better Than the Nation, and Still Feels Tight

Redfin's same data lists $100,925 as the income needed nationally, so Chico asks for less. But local incomes are lower too, which is why so many Chico households still find the math hard at today's rates.

What Is the Monthly Payment on a $479,000 Chico Home?

At a 7.18 percent rate and 10 percent down, principal and interest on a $479,000 home is about $2,920 per month. Chico homes have been selling near $479,000, taking an average of about 49 days to sell, and the 30-year fixed rate averaged 7.18 percent in Freddie Mac's November 5 survey.

How Much Cash Do You Need to Buy in Chico?

Plan for your down payment plus closing costs and inspections. On a $479,000 home, 5 percent down is about $23,950 and 10 percent is about $47,900, before closing costs that commonly run 2 to 5 percent of the price. Some loan programs and down payment assistance options can reduce the cash you need up front, and our guide to First-Time Home Buyer Guide: Chico, CA 2026 covers where to start. Is Renting Cheaper Than Buying in Chico?

Month to month, renting usually costs less. The Census Bureau lists median gross rent in Chico at $1,442, while principal and interest alone on a median-priced home is near $2,920. Only 44.3 percent of Chico homes are owner-occupied, which tells you how many households rent here.

Buying makes sense when you plan to stay long enough for equity to build and you want a payment that is not tied to a landlord's rent increases. If you are weighing the two, Rent vs. Buy in Chico walks through the tradeoffs.

How Can You Lower the Income You Need to Buy?

Several levers can bring the required income down, and a good plan often combines more than one:

  • Put more down, or use a down payment assistance program if you qualify, to shrink the loan.
  • Pay down credit cards and car loans before applying, since monthly debts count against your income.
  • Ask for a seller credit toward closing costs or a rate buydown when the market allows it.
  • Add a co-borrower whose income helps you qualify, if that fits your situation.
  • Look at nearby towns where prices run lower, such as Orland, Oroville, and Red Bluff.
